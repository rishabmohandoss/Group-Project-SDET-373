MISE Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Step Counter uses account information and wellness metrics to provide personal health and fitness insights. We do not sell personal information or use health information for advertising.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. Demo accounts also contain fictional age, sex, weight, device, and food preferences. In an iPhone development build, choosing Read and save to my account requests permission to read steps, resting heart rate, and sleep from Apple Health. A summary with source names and timestamps is saved to your account in Back4App; raw samples are not uploaded. Sync is manual and replaces the previous summary.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, save your profile, show your progress, and generate wellness recommendations. We do not diagnose, treat, or prevent medical conditions.

Your choices

You can remove saved health readings from Vitals or delete your account and its profile and health summary from the You tab. Manage MISE permissions in Apple Health to revoke access on your device.
